# Scenario and problem

## Setting

A shared clinic workstation in a hospital. Several clinicians use one computer during a shift. The machine sits at a desk, not at a patient bed.

## What goes wrong

Staff are called to a bed or a corridor. The session often stays open because locking it takes time. The next person, or a visitor, can see the last screen. A check that depends on the cloud does not run when the ward network drops.

## Who is affected

Clinicians who share the computer, and the patients whose records are on that screen. DeskSide does not identify either of them.

## How the ward copes today

People rely on remembering to lock the screen. There is no local check that the chair is empty while the session is still open.

## What DeskSide changes

A desk camera and, if the team is shortlisted, an ASUS UGen300 USB accelerator decide only whether a person is at the chair. If the person has left and the session is open, the PC shows a lock prompt. A staff member confirms. The tool does not close a clinical record by itself.
