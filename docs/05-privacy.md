# Privacy

The camera looks at a chair in a workplace. The design follows two limits.

## Ghana Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843)

- The purpose is only to know whether a person is at the workstation.
- Frames are minimised: processed on the PC, not kept for a later upload.
- The model does not read a name, a folder number, or a diagnosis.

## Session lock

NIST SP 800-53 Revision 5, control AC-11, is the pattern: lock the session when the user leaves. DeskSide supplies the “has the person left?” signal on the PC itself, because the ward network is not reliable.

## Operating rules

- Staff can turn the camera off. The tool does not override that.
- Test recordings for the contest are of the builder, or of an empty chair.
- No patient, no second person’s face, and no clinical record appears in the repository or in the demo video.
- The local log, if kept, stores time and present or absent. It does not store the frame.
